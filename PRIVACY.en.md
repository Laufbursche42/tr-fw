# Privacy

The short version: this page collects nothing. No sign-in, no cookies, no tracking pixels, no analytics, no ads.

## Your firmware file

The file you pick is **read and processed in the browser**. It is not uploaded, not cached and not sent to any server. The firmware you build is produced on your own device and lands in your file system as a download.

You can check this: switch off your network connection once the page has loaded. Building still works.

The **Check your EEPROM** button connects over Bluetooth from your own browser directly to your own scooter, so no reading it shows is sent to any server.

## Hosting

The page is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs requests to its servers, which usually includes the IP address, the time and the address requested. We have no influence over that and we do not see those logs.

What GitHub does with them is set out in the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

## Links to other sites

This page links to GitHub repositories. Once you follow a link, that site's own terms apply.

## If you open a ticket

A ticket is a public GitHub issue. Your user name, your text and anything you attach are publicly visible. Do not attach anything you would not want published, in particular nothing that identifies you or your vehicle.
